# Pricing — Trippy

## Free (the only plan)

- **Price:** $0/month — completely free
- **Users per trip:** Unlimited
- **Trips:** Unlimited
- **Features included:**
  - Day-by-day shared itinerary
  - Group budget tracking and expense splitting
  - Live interactive trip map
  - Shared packing list (synced in real time)
  - Invite crew via shareable link
  - AI trip helper (place ideas, budget tips, packing advice)
  - Real-time sync across all group members
  - Progressive Web App — works on any browser or device

- **What's required:** Google account to sign in (name and email only)
- **Credit card:** Not required — ever

Trippy is free with no premium tier, no usage limits, and no hidden costs.
