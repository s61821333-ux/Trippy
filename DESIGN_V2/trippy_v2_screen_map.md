# Trippy v2: Full Screen Map & Innovation Blueprint

## 1. Onboarding & Identity
- **01 · Welcome (Done):** Cinematic entry point.
- **02 · Sign In / Join:** Ultra-clean glass login with biometrics.
- **03 · Trip Creation:** Step-by-step flow (Name -> Dates -> Vibe).
- **04 · Theme Picker:** Interactive "jelly" bubbles for selecting visual themes (Desert, Alpine, Urban).
- **05 · Invite Crew:** Visual "share sheet" with group avatar previews.

## 2. Planning & Logistics
- **06 · Trip Dashboard (Done):** High-level journey map.
- **07 · Day Timeline (Done):** Vertical feed with "gap analysis."
- **08 · Add Event Sheet:** Innovative category selector using the "emblem" language.
- **09 · Packing List:** Responsive checklist with weight/priority indicators.
- **10 · Supply Inventory:** Categorized view of group gear (Who is bringing what).

## 3. Exploration & Discovery
- **11 · World Map:** Full-screen immersive map with custom brand terrain.
- **12 · Discovery Engine:** AI-powered "Fly-over" view of suggested spots.
- **13 · Place Details:** Glass card with reviews, hours, and "Trip Vibe" compatibility.
- **14 · Route Optimizer:** Visual comparison of different travel paths.

## 4. Social & Memories
- **15 · Group Chat:** In-app coordination with event-tagging.
- **16 · Photo Journal:** Collaborative album tied to specific timeline nodes.
- **17 · Post-Trip Summary:** "Sticker-style" infographic of the journey.

## 5. System & Utility
- **18 · User Profile:** Travel stats, badges, and past expeditions.
- **19 · Settings:** Deep config for map layers, notifications, and privacy.
- **20 · Global Search:** Instant cross-trip search for places and notes.

---

## Innovation Details
- **Specular Highlights:** All buttons feature dynamic highlights that follow the device tilt.
- **Liquid Morphing:** Transitions between screens use a "merging drop" effect.
- **Brand Emblems:** Moving away from standard icons to the "28 Tiny Compasses" emblem system defined in the Brand Assets.
- **Depth Layers:** Background landscapes are multi-plane parallax scenes that reflect local weather.
